Four scenes from the journey

A story that crosses
centuries, seas and traditions.

Explore four defining moments from the book, from Alicante and Nicholas's childhood to the Dutch tradition of Sinterklaas.

01 · Alicante, the unexpected turn

What if Saint Nicholas—the first Santa Claus—lived in Spain?

The story turns Alicante harbour into the starting point of a Christmas adventure that connects Spain with the origin of a universal legend.

02 · Patara, long before the legend

Discover the story of Saint Nicholas of Bari from his youth.

Before the mitre, the ship and the fame, there was a child who learned that the greatest wealth was sharing and helping those in need.

03 · Two names joined by Christmas

Discover how one story gave rise to another.

See what connects Saint Nicholas and Santa Claus, what sets them apart and how one man's generosity crossed centuries and borders.

04 · The Dutch tradition

Discover the Dutch tradition of Sinterklaas and the Pieten.

In Dutch tradition, Saint Nicholas lives in Spain. This story imagines him setting sail each year from Alicante harbour with the young Pieten to deliver joy over rooftops and down chimneys.

About the story

From Saint Nicholas
to Santa Claus.

The story begins with Saint Nicholas and introduces readers to the origins of a tradition that would, over time, inspire the figure of Santa Claus.

Through an illustrated adventure, the book explores generosity, helping others and the way a story can travel across places and generations without losing its magic.

✦A story made for family reading

Tradition, imagination and positive values come together in an illustrated story that preserves the wonder of Christmas.

Yes. The Spanish edition is available from Amazon in hardback and Kindle formats.

Which formats are available?

You can buy the hardback or read the Kindle edition. The printed edition preserves the double-page illustrations.

Yes. A separate English edition is planned, but it is not yet available.

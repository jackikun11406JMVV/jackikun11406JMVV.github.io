Who was Saint Nicholas?

Long before Santa Claus existed, there was a man named Saint Nicholas, known for his generosity and for secretly helping those who needed it most. Over the centuries, his story travelled across Europe and inspired different traditions before becoming the character we know today.

This upcoming children's book tells that historical journey through illustrations and a family-friendly approach, showing how a real story inspired one of Christmas's most famous figures.

What will you find in this book?

  • The story of Saint Nicholas told for children.
  • The origin of Santa Claus through history and tradition.
  • Original illustrations.
  • Curiosities about how the legend evolved.

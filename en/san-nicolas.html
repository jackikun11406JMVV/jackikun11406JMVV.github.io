About the story

From Saint Nicholas
to Santa Claus.

The story begins with Saint Nicholas and introduces readers to the origins of a tradition that would, over time, inspire the figure of Santa Claus.

Through an illustrated adventure, the book explores generosity, helping others and the way a story can travel across places and generations without losing its magic.

✦A story made for family reading

Tradition, imagination and positive values come together in an illustrated story that preserves the wonder of Christmas.

A tradition passed down from generation to generation.

The Little Mouse Pérez is part of children's imagination and a tradition many families share with their children when a tooth falls out.

The literary tradition of the character is linked to Luis Coloma, a figure who is part of the history of the Little Mouse Pérez and how the character has been passed down through generations.

But when a child asks “Where does the Little Mouse Pérez come from?”, they are often not looking only for a date or a name. They are looking for a story. They want to know how Pérez can visit so many homes, how he knows all the children and why each family experiences the magic in its own way.

The Story Behind the Book

And then,
Juanito Pérez was born in Jerez.

This is the magical literary interpretation imagined by Juan Manuel Vela Vacas for his book.

In The Origin of Little Mouse Pérez, the story begins in Jerez de la Frontera. Among wineries, barrels and the atmosphere of a city full of tradition, we meet a little mouse who is about to become someone very special.

It is a story created to help children imagine answers to the many questions they ask when they lose a tooth.

Questions Children Ask

What Will Children Learn from This Book?

With this book, children can find answers to many of the questions they may have when the Little Mouse Pérez comes to their home. Through imagination and magic, the story accompanies those questions and helps make sense of them.

The story explores questions such as:

How can the Little Mouse Pérez reach every home in the world? Does the Tooth Fairy and the Little Mouse Pérez really exist? How can he be in several places at the same time? Where does he come from? What is his story?

And also those small differences that children notice so much: why does my Little Mouse Pérez leave sweets while my friend gets money? Why do I leave my tooth in a glass while my friend leaves it under the pillow?

Many of these answers are within the pages of The Origin of Little Mouse Pérez.

Questions About the Author

Who is Juan Manuel Vela Vacas?

Juan Manuel Vela Vacas is the author of The Origin of Little Mouse Pérez and Jara, the Noble PDD. Learn more about the author →

Where was the Little Mouse Pérez born?

In Juan Manuel Vela Vacas' literary version, the Little Mouse Pérez was born in Jerez de la Frontera.

How did you come up with the idea for this book?

After hearing that Father Luis Coloma, author of the first literary description of the Little Mouse Pérez, was born in Jerez, Juan Manuel began to imagine… what if, when he was young, Coloma had met some friendly winery mice there, including the famous Pérez? That question gave birth to the legend that inspires this story.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who gave Little Mouse Pérez his best-known literary identity?

Luis Coloma gave the character his best-known literary identity in a story written for the young King Alfonso XIII. The name Ratón Pérez appears in earlier references, although without the modern tradition of exchanging teeth for gifts.

Is Little Mouse Pérez the Spanish Tooth Fairy?

Yes. In Spain, Little Mouse Pérez fulfils the same role as the Tooth Fairy in many English-speaking countries.

Is this the original story?

No. This book is an original fictional interpretation inspired by the classic Spanish tradition.

Who is this book for?

It is written for children, families and anyone interested in discovering one of Spain's most famous literary traditions.

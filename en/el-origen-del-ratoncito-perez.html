Who is Little Mouse Pérez?

While many English-speaking countries know the Tooth Fairy, children in Spain traditionally wait for Little Mouse Pérez. Instead of a fairy collecting lost baby teeth, a small and kind mouse visits children during the night, leaving a small gift or coin in exchange for each tooth.

This beloved Spanish tradition has been part of family life for generations. The Origin of the Little Mouse Pérez reimagines how this famous character could have begun his journey, blending Spanish culture, Jerez de la Frontera and imagination into a new children's adventure.

Frequently Asked Questions

Who created Little Mouse Pérez?

The literary character was created by the Spanish writer Luis Coloma in the late nineteenth century for the young King Alfonso XIII.

Is Little Mouse Pérez the Spanish Tooth Fairy?

Yes. In Spain, Little Mouse Pérez fulfils the same role as the Tooth Fairy in many English-speaking countries.

Is this the original story?

No. This book is an original fictional interpretation inspired by the classic Spanish tradition.

Who is this book for?

It is written for children, families and anyone interested in discovering one of Spain's most famous literary traditions.
